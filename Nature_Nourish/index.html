About Us

The idea of sustainable eating was first formalized by the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) of the United Nations, which introduced the modern definition of “sustainable diets” in 2010. Their work emphasized foods that are healthy, environmentally responsible, and culturally respectful. The concept was strengthened by the World Health Organization (WHO) through research on healthy, plant-forward diets, and by decades of environmental studies highlighting the impact of food production on the planet. Later, global scientists and groups like the EAT-Lancet Commission helped popularize sustainable eating worldwide. Together, these organizations shaped today’s understanding of how our food choices affect both health and the environment. Sustainable eating means choosing foods that are healthy for your body and have a low impact on the environment. This includes plant-based diets, seasonal foods, reducing food waste, and supporting local farmers.
