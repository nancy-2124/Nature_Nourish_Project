Our Sustainable Recipes

Roasted Veggie Chickpea Bowl

Ingredients

  • Chickpeas
  • Seasonal veggies (carrot, capsicum, pumpkin)
  • Salt
  • Turmeric
  • Chilli
  • Millets/Rice
  • Lemon/Chutney

Steps

  1. Mix chickpeas & veggies with oil and spices.
  2. Roast or pan-fry till soft & slightly crispy.
  3. Serve over millets/rice with lemon or chutney.

Tofu Quino Veggie Bowl

Ingredients

  • Cooked quinoa
  • Firm tofu (cubed)
  • Sweet potato (cubed)
  • Broccoli
  • Zucchini
  • Olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, chilli flakes
  • Garlic or paprika

Steps

  1. Toss veggies + tofu with olive oil, salt, pepper, spices.
  2. Roast or pan-fry till golden and tender.
  3. Add cooked quinoa to a bowl, top with roasted tofu & veggies.
  4. Serve warm (lemon/sesame seeds optional).

Quino and Black Bean Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • ½ cup boiled black beans
  • 1 small cucumber (chopped)
  • 1 tomato (chopped)
  • 2 tbsp onion (optional)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Pinch of cumin/chilli
  • Coriander/mint

Steps

  1. Mix quinoa, beans, and chopped veggies in a bowl.
  2. Add lemon juice, olive oil, salt, pepper (and spices).
  3. Toss well and serve fresh (or chill for 10–15 min).

Lentil Soup

Ingredients

  • ½ cup lentils (masoor/moong/toor)
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 1 tomato (chopped)
  • 1 carrot or any veggie (optional)
  • 1–2 garlic cloves
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • Salt & pepper
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Water

Steps

  1. Add all ingredients to one pot with water.
  2. Cook till lentils and veggies are soft.
  3. Lightly mash or blend (optional).
  4. Serve hot (lemon optional).

Broccoli and Mushroom Stir Fry

Ingredients

  • Broccoli florets
  • Mushrooms (sliced)
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 1–2 garlic cloves (chopped)
  • Soy sauce or salt
  • Black pepper / chilli flakes (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat oil, sauté garlic.
  2. Add broccoli & mushrooms, stir-fry on high heat 3–5 mins.
  3. Add soy sauce/salt and pepper.
  4. Toss and serve hot.
